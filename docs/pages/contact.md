# Contact Page Requirements

Last updated: 2026-08-31

## Page identity

- Public route: `contact.html`
- Product role: provide a clear way to contact / reach ONDesign
- Shared implementation family: `src/features/info/info.css`

## Page goal

Help visitors find the correct contact or project communication channels with minimal friction.

## Core user task

A visitor should be able to identify the right way to reach ONDesign, understand what each channel is for, and continue without navigating through unrelated product content.

## Core functions

- Present the supported contact methods.
- Keep external links accurate and understandable.
- Support Chinese and English versions.

## Information structure

1. Short contact context
2. Available contact / social / project channels
3. Any concise response expectation or usage note if needed

## Interaction rules

- Keep contact actions direct and obvious.
- External destinations should be clearly distinguishable and use the correct destination.
- Avoid long forms or multi-step contact flows unless explicitly required by a future product decision.
- Keep Chinese and English content structurally equivalent.

## Keep

- Simple, direct contact information.
- Shared global App Shell.

## Remove / avoid

- Unrelated product marketing sections.
- Launcher or Library functionality.
- Long forms unless explicitly required by a future product decision.

## Modification boundary

This page only handles contact and communication entry points. Broader product explanation belongs on About; product workflows stay on their dedicated pages.
