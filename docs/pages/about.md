# About Page Requirements

Last updated: 2026-08-31

## Page identity

- Public route: `about.html`
- Product role: explain ONDesign and its purpose
- Shared implementation family: `src/features/info/info.css`

## Page goal

Give visitors a concise understanding of what ONDesign is, why it exists and what kind of design workflow it supports.

## Core functions

- Explain the project purpose.
- Clarify the relationship between AI Coding, UI references and Design DNA.
- Provide relevant navigation to core product areas.
- Support Chinese and English versions.

## Information structure

1. What ONDesign is
2. Why it exists
3. What users can do with it
4. Relevant next links

## Keep

- Concise product explanation.
- Shared global App Shell.
- Clear links to core product flows.

## Remove / avoid

- Product configuration controls.
- Full case or resource directories.
- Long internal development history.

## Modification boundary

This page explains the product; it does not perform the main product workflows. Functional design tools belong on their dedicated pages.
