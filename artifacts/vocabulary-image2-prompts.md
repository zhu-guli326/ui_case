# Vocabulary image2 prompts

Shared constraints for every sheet: clean editorial still life, soft daylight, restrained
cobalt/neutral/coral/mint palette, crisp material detail, no readable text, no logo, no
watermark, no icons, no UI symbols, no status bar, no arrows, no gear, no menu dots, no
plus/minus, no power symbol, no playback controls, no tab icons, no toggles, no status dots.

## Page foundations

A seamless 3x2 contact sheet of five distinct architectural paper-and-glass scenes that
suggest shell, header, hero, content section, and footer. Each cell must work as an isolated
16:10 editorial cover and have no labels or separators.

## Navigation and discovery

A seamless 3x2 contact sheet of six distinct physical wayfinding scenes using paths,
compasses without glyphs, shelves, index cards, and spatial landmarks. Each cell is an
isolated 16:10 cover with no labels or separators.

## Content display

A seamless 3x2 contact sheet of five distinct editorial scenes suggesting cards, grids,
lists, media tiles, and tables through photography and paper objects. No embedded UI.

## Controls and forms

A seamless 3x1 contact sheet of three tactile scenes using blank paper fields, sliders as
physical rails, and restrained control surfaces. No actual UI glyphs or readable labels.

## Feedback and overlays

A seamless 2x2 contact sheet of four layered translucent-paper scenes suggesting modal,
drawer, toast, and state feedback. No embedded UI, labels, or symbols.

## Visual realization

A single cinematic macro still life of materializing pixels, frosted glass, paper texture,
and cobalt light, composed as a 16:10 editorial cover with no text or interface chrome.
